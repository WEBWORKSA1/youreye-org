## Not medical advice

The content, checks, calculators, quizzes and videos on YourEye.org are for general information and education only. They are **not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment**. Always seek the advice of an optometrist, ophthalmologist or other qualified health provider with any questions about your eyes or vision.

## Screening checks are not eye exams

Our online checks are screening aids. Results depend on your screen, its settings, lighting, distance and other factors, and they can be wrong. A normal result does not mean your eyes are healthy. Many serious eye diseases, including glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy, have no early symptoms and can only be found with a comprehensive eye exam.

## Emergencies

If you have sudden vision loss, flashes of light, a sudden increase in floaters, a curtain or shadow over your vision, severe eye pain, a chemical in your eye or an eye injury, **seek emergency care immediately**. Do not use this website or the matching service for emergencies.

## No doctor-patient relationship

Using YourEye.org, including the matching service, does not create a doctor-patient relationship with YourEye.org. Providers you are matched with are independent and solely responsible for the care they provide. We do not endorse or guarantee any provider, product or procedure.

## Accuracy

We work to keep information accurate and current, but medical knowledge changes. We make no warranty that the information is complete, current or error-free. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read here.
