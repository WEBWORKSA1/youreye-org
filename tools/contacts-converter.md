## The vertex distance formula

A lens has a different effect depending on how far it sits from the eye. The converter uses the standard formula *Fc = F / (1 − d × F)*, where F is the glasses power and d is the vertex distance in metres. For minus lenses, the contact lens power is weaker than the glasses; for plus lenses it is stronger.

For astigmatism, each main direction (meridian) is converted separately, then turned back into sphere and cylinder. For small amounts of astigmatism, a spherical lens using the *spherical equivalent* often works well.

## Why you still need a fitting

Contact lenses rest on the cornea, so their curve and size must match your eye. A poorly fitting lens can cut off oxygen to the cornea, scratch it or move around and blur vision. Contact lens wearers also have a higher risk of serious eye infections, so follow the [contact lens safety guide](guides/contact-lens-safety.html).
