## About dry eye

Dry eye happens when your eyes do not make enough tears, or when the tears evaporate too fast, often because the oil glands in the eyelids are blocked. It is one of the most common reasons people visit an eye doctor, and it becomes more common with age, screen use, contact lens wear, menopause and some medicines.

## Reading your score

Your score runs from 0 to 40. Under 8 suggests minimal symptoms; 8 to 15 mild; 16 to 25 moderate; above 25 marked. The questions focus on how often symptoms happen, since frequent symptoms matter more than an occasional gritty day.

## Treatments eye doctors use

- Preservative-free artificial tears and gels
- Warm compresses and lid hygiene for blocked oil glands
- Prescription anti-inflammatory drops
- Punctal plugs to keep tears on the eye longer
- In-office treatments for meibomian gland dysfunction

A red, painful eye, light sensitivity or a sudden drop in vision is not typical dry eye; get it checked the same day.
