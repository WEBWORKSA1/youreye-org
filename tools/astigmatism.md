## How the chart works

The chart has groups of lines pointing in every direction, like the hours on a clock. An eye without astigmatism focuses all directions equally, so every group looks equally black. An eye with astigmatism focuses one direction better than the one at right angles to it, so some groups look darker, sharper or thicker.

Try it first without glasses. If you wear glasses that correct astigmatism, the lines should look even when you put them back on.

## Limits of a screen check

Small amounts of astigmatism may not show on a screen, and the result depends on distance and screen sharpness. An eye exam measures astigmatism precisely, including its strength (the CYL number on a prescription) and direction (the AXIS).

## Treatment

Astigmatism is corrected with glasses, toric soft contact lenses, rigid gas-permeable lenses, or laser procedures such as LASIK, PRK and SMILE. During cataract surgery, a toric lens implant can correct it too.
