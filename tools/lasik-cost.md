## How the calculator works

Enter what you spend each year on glasses, contact lenses and solution. The calculator multiplies that by the years until the age you choose, then compares it with the LASIK price for one or both eyes. If you plan to finance, it shows the monthly payment and total cost with interest.

Routine eye exams are left out because you still need them after LASIK. Reading glasses are also left out: most people need them from their mid-40s whether or not they had surgery.

## What to ask when comparing quotes

- Is the price per eye or for both eyes?
- Which technology is included, and does the price change with my prescription?
- Are follow-up visits and any enhancement included, and for how long?
- Is there a financing offer, and what is the APR after any promotional period?

## Cost is not the only factor

Choose a surgeon based on experience, a thorough screening and honest advice about whether you are a good candidate. A clinic that tells you LASIK is not right for you is doing its job.
