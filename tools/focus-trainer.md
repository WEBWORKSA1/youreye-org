## What the routine does

1. **Palming** rests the eyes in darkness and warmth and helps you slow down.
2. **Full blinks** spread the tear film. During screen work people blink less often, and many blinks are incomplete.
3. **Near and far focusing** lets the focusing muscle relax after holding close focus for a long time.
4. **Smooth tracking** moves the eyes through their range while your head stays still.
5. **Rest** with eyes closed to finish.

## An honest note

The internet is full of claims that eye exercises can throw away your glasses. The evidence does not support that for refractive errors. Vision therapy prescribed by an eye doctor does help specific problems, such as convergence insufficiency, where the eyes struggle to work together up close.

If your eyes stay tired, blurry or uncomfortable after regular breaks, check for dry eye and have your prescription reviewed.
