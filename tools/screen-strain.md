## What the score measures

The calculator weighs the factors most linked to digital eye strain: total screen hours, how often you take breaks, viewing distance and height, brightness, glare, dry air, and whether your glasses are right for screen distance. It then ranks the changes that would make the biggest difference for you.

## The ideal screen setup

- **Distance:** about an arm's length (50 to 75 cm) from your eyes
- **Height:** top of the screen at or just below eye level, so you look slightly down
- **Light:** screen brightness matched to the room, no bright window behind or in front
- **Text:** large enough to read without leaning in
- **Breaks:** the 20-20-20 rule, plus a proper break every couple of hours

## When to see an eye doctor

If strain continues after fixing your setup, you may have dry eye, uncorrected astigmatism, early presbyopia or a problem with how your eyes work together. Many people benefit from glasses designed for screen distance.
