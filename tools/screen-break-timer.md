## How to use the timer

Press **Start** and keep this tab open while you work. Every 20 minutes the timer chimes and, if you allow it, shows a desktop notification. Look at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for 20 seconds and blink fully a few times. The timer then counts the next 20 minutes on its own and tallies your breaks for the day.

You can change the interval, mute the chime, or pause the timer for meetings. Your settings are saved in this browser.

## Why screens tire your eyes

Looking at something close keeps the focusing muscles inside your eyes working, and people blink much less when concentrating on a screen. Hours of that leads to tired, dry, sore or blurry eyes and headaches, often called *digital eye strain* or computer vision syndrome.

## Other habits that help

- Keep the screen about an arm's length away, slightly below eye level
- Match screen brightness to the room and cut glare
- Enlarge text instead of leaning in
- Use lubricating drops if your eyes feel dry
- Get your eyes checked; many people over 40 need a separate prescription for screen distance
