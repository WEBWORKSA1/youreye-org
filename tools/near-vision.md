## What this check measures

Near vision is measured with reading cards printed in standard *N* sizes, where the number is the print size in points. Newspaper text is about N8 to N10; the fine print on a medicine label is often N6 or smaller. Holding the screen at 40 cm, the smallest line you can read comfortably shows how well your eyes focus up close.

For accurate print sizes, calibrate your screen first in step 1 of the [visual acuity check](tools/acuity.html). The size is saved on your device for this check too.

## Presbyopia: the reason most people need readers

The lens inside the eye gradually stiffens with age, and by the early to mid 40s most people notice they hold menus and phones farther away. This is *presbyopia*. It happens to everyone, including people who have never needed glasses, and it keeps progressing until about age 60.

## Options for clear near vision

- Ready-made reading glasses for people with good distance vision
- Prescription readers, bifocals or progressive lenses
- Multifocal or monovision contact lenses
- Prescription eye drops that temporarily shrink the pupil
- Monovision laser correction or lens replacement surgery for some people
