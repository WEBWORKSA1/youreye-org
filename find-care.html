Free eye-care matching

Find the right eye care near you

Tell us what you need in about two minutes. We pass your request to up to three local providers who offer that service, and they contact you to answer questions and book a visit.

  • Free for you
  • About 2 minutes
  • Up to 3 local providers
  • No obligation to book

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need
    Service, location and the best way to reach you.
  2. We match you
    With providers that offer that service near you.
  3. They contact you
    Compare answers and prices, then decide.

Who we can connect you with

LASIK, SMILE, PRK and ICL surgery centers; cataract surgeons; optometrists for routine and contact lens exams; pediatric eye care and myopia-control clinics; dry eye clinics; glaucoma and retina specialists; opticians; and low-vision services.

How we make money, honestly

Providers may pay YourEye.org a fee for introductions or to be featured. That never costs you anything. We share your request only with the providers you are matched with, and we do not sell your details to advertisers or data brokers. See our privacy policy.

Is this an emergency?

For sudden vision loss, a curtain over your vision, new flashes or floaters, a chemical in the eye or a painful red eye, do not wait for a match. Call an eye doctor now or go to an emergency department. Eye emergencies guide
