Support us

Keep eye checks free for everyone

At least a billion people worldwide live with a vision problem that could have been prevented or has not been treated, according to the World Health Organization. Many simply never get checked. YourEye.org gives anyone a free first step. Your support keeps it free and growing.

Where your support goes

Operations

Hosting, tools, accessibility testing and keeping every check free and ad-light.

Promotions and outreach

Reaching people who have never had an eye exam, including awareness-month campaigns.

Hiring talent

Paying eye-care professionals to review content, and writers, translators and video creators.

Contests and prizes

Monthly Eye-Q prizes that make learning about eye health fun.
