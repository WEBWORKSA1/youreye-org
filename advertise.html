Our advertising standards

  • All paid placements are clearly labeled as sponsored or featured.
  • Sponsors never influence medical content, check results or condition pages.
  • No claims that a product cures or prevents disease without regulatory approval, no before-and-after guarantees, and no prescription-only products marketed directly where the law does not allow it.
  • Display ads are served by Google AdSense under Google's publisher policies. We do not place ads inside lead forms.
  • We may decline any advertiser.
