--- title: YourEye.org description: Free at-home eye checks, plain-English guides to eye conditions and a free service that matches you with eye-care providers near you. body_class: home faq: - q: Is YourEye.org free? a: Yes. Every check, guide and video is free and needs no account. The matching service is also free for you; providers may pay us for introductions, and ads keep the rest of the site running. - q: Can an online eye check replace an eye exam? a: No. Our checks are screening aids that can flag changes worth looking into. Only an in-person exam by an optometrist or ophthalmologist can measure your prescription accurately and check eye health, including diseases like glaucoma that have no early symptoms. - q: Where do my check results go? a: Results are calculated in your browser and are not sent to us. We only receive information you choose to submit through a form, such as a request to be matched with a provider. - q: How does the matching service work? a: You tell us what you need, where you are and how to reach you. We pass your request to up to three local providers that offer that service, and they contact you. You decide whether to book. There is no cost or obligation. ---
