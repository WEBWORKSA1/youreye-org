Official rules

No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not improve your chances.

  1. Organizer. The contests are run by the operator of YourEye.org (\"Organizer\"). Contact: our contact form.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals who are 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live, if higher. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees and contractors of the Organizer and any prize sponsor, and their immediate families, may not enter.
  3. Entry period. Each contest runs for one calendar month, from 00:00 on the first day to 23:59 on the last day (US Eastern Time). Entries outside the period are not counted.
  4. How to enter. Quiz draw: complete the monthly Eye-Q quiz with a score of 7 or more and submit the entry form. Creative contest: submit the creative entry form. Limit one quiz entry and one creative entry per person per month. Alternative free entry: send your name, email, country and the words \"Eye-Q free entry\" through our contact form during the entry period.
  5. Winner selection. The quiz winner is drawn at random from all eligible entries within 14 days after the month closes. The creative winner is chosen by a panel using the published criteria. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries.
  6. Prizes. The prize is shown on this page for that month. No cash alternative. The Organizer may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a prize becomes unavailable. Prizes are not transferable. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  7. Notification. Winners are notified by email and must reply within 7 days, and may need to confirm eligibility. Otherwise an alternate winner may be selected. Skill-testing questions may be required where the law requires them.
  8. Publicity. With the winner's permission, the Organizer may publish the winner's first name, country and creative entry.
  9. Content licence. Creative entrants keep their copyright and grant the Organizer a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display the entry on YourEye.org and its newsletter and social channels, with credit.
  10. Privacy. Entry details are used to run the contest and, if you opt in, to send the digest. See the privacy policy.
  11. General. The Organizer may disqualify entries that are fraudulent, automated or break these rules, and may cancel or modify a contest if it cannot run as planned. These contests are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any social media platform.
