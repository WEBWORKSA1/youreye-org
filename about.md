## Why we built YourEye.org

According to the World Health Organization, at least **2.2 billion people** worldwide have a near or distance vision impairment, and in at least **1 billion** of them it could have been prevented or has not yet been addressed. Lost productivity from vision impairment is estimated at **US$411 billion a year**. A large share of that is simple to fix: an eye exam and a pair of glasses, or catching a disease like glaucoma early.

Many people put off eye care because they are unsure whether their problem is worth a visit, what tests mean or where to go. YourEye.org exists to make that first step easy.

## What we offer

- **[Free eye checks](tools/)**: sixteen interactive screening checks and calculators, from visual acuity and the Amsler grid to a LASIK candidacy quiz and a prescription decoder. Results are calculated in your browser.
- **[Plain-English condition guides](conditions/)** and **[in-depth guides](guides/)**, written from the guidance of the National Eye Institute, American Academy of Ophthalmology, American Optometric Association, CDC, FDA and WHO, with sources on every page.
- **[A free matching service](find-care.html)** that connects you with local eye-care providers when you are ready for an exam, a consultation or treatment.
- **[Official videos](videos.html)**, [monthly contests](contests.html) and a monthly digest.

## What we are not

We are not a clinic and we do not give medical advice or diagnoses. Our checks are screening aids. Only an in-person exam by a qualified eye-care professional can diagnose and treat eye conditions. Read our [medical disclaimer](disclaimer.html).

We are also not a charity. YourEye.org is an independent, ad-supported website.

## How the site is funded

- **Advertising** served by Google AdSense, clearly separated from content
- **Introductions** to eye-care providers, who may pay a fee when we match them with someone who asked for care
- **Featured listings and sponsorships**, always labeled
- **Affiliate links** to some eyewear retailers, which may earn a commission at no cost to you
- **[Support from readers](support.html)**

None of these influence what our condition pages say or how our checks score. See our [editorial policy](editorial-policy.html).
