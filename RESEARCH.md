# YourEye.org: concept and research

## The decision

**YourEye.org = free at-home eye checks + a plain-English eye-health library + an eye-care matching service.**

Tools pull the traffic. The library earns trust and AdSense impressions. The matching form ("Find my eye-care match") is the revenue engine: every tool result and article ends with a route into it.

"Your eye" reads as personal and first-person. That fits a self-check site ("check your eye") better than a magazine. The `.org` reads as public-interest, which helps trust, donations and partnerships. It also creates an obligation to be transparent that the site is an independent, ad-supported website and not a charity. The disclosure page says so.

## Why this niche pays

| Signal | Number | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People with a near or distance vision impairment | 2.2 billion; at least 1 billion preventable or unaddressed | WHO fact sheet |
| Productivity lost to vision impairment | US$411 billion a year | WHO fact sheet |
| LASIK click prices in competitive metros | $20 to $80 per click | Patient10x via Specialty Vision, 2026 |
| What clinics pay per qualified lead | LASIK $90 to $250; cataract $40 to $120 | Specialty Vision benchmarks, 2026 |
| Ophthalmology / LASIK average lead cost | $70 to $140 | MedicalMarketingFirm CPL tool |
| AdSense RPM, health and wellness | $5 to $12 (US page RPM $8 to $18) | EcomCalcTools; WeForAds 2026 |
| Eyewear affiliate commission | EyeBuyDirect up to 15% (AOV about $70); Zenni up to 12% | Retailer affiliate pages |

The gap that matters: an advertiser pays $20 to $80 for one LASIK click, but a publisher's AdSense RPM is only $5 to $12 per 1,000 pageviews. Selling the lead directly captures far more of that value than showing the ad.

## Revenue model at month 12 (assumptions, not promises)

50,000 visits a month at 2.4 pages per visit = 120,000 pageviews.

| Stream | Assumption | Monthly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdSense | 120k pageviews x $6 RPM | about $720 |
| Leads, high value (LASIK, cataract, myopia control) | 0.8% of visits convert = 400 leads; 35% high value = 140 x $60 | about $8,400 |
| Leads, routine (exams, glasses, dry eye) | 260 x $8 | about $2,080 |
| Eyewear affiliate | 0.4% buy = 200 x $70 x 10% | about $1,400 |
| Sponsorship, featured clinics | 3 x $149 | about $450 |

Halve the lead line for buyer churn and it is still about 7x AdSense. **Conclusion: lead generation is the business; AdSense is the floor.**

### Safe path vs bold path

- **Safe:** content, tools, AdSense and eyewear affiliates. Collect leads but only route them once 2 to 3 clinics per metro have signed up. Low risk, slow money.
- **Bold:** sell an exclusive LASIK-and-cataract lead feed to one clinic per metro on a $1,000 to $3,000 monthly retainer, backed by "LASIK cost in [city]" pages for the top 50 US and Canadian metros. Faster money, but it needs sales calls and a reviewer with credentials before Google will rank the money pages.

### Flaws to fix early

1. **YMYL (Your Money or Your Life).** Google holds health pages to a higher bar. Recruit one optometrist or ophthalmologist reviewer early; the Careers page is set up for this. Until then, every page cites NEI, AAO, AOA, WHO or CDC and says it is not medical advice.
2. **Leads without buyers are worth $0.** The For Providers page and the lead scoring exist so the first clinic can be signed before traffic arrives.
3. **AdSense will not approve a github.io subdomain.** Point youreye.org at GitHub Pages before applying.
4. **Trademark.** "YOUR EYES" is a live US registration (Reg. 7307462) owned by the International Agency for the Prevention of Blindness, covering classes 35, 36, 41 and 44, and IAPB runs the "Love Your Eyes" campaign. "MYEYES" (Reg. 7890786, class 44) is also live. The site therefore always brands as the domain **YourEye.org**, never "Your Eyes" or "Love Your Eyes", and the disclosure page says it is unaffiliated.

### Features adopted

- **Tools hub** (16 tools): acuity screener, Amsler grid, color vision, astigmatism dial, contrast, near-vision card, 20-20-20 timer, focus and blink trainer, dry-eye score, screen-strain risk, prescription decoder, glasses-to-contacts converter, LASIK candidacy quiz, LASIK lifetime cost, kids' myopia risk, exam schedule by age.
- **Condition library** using the Mayo-style structure, with sources and a "when to get help now" box on every page.
- **Two-step-plus lead form** (NVISION/TLC pattern) with consent, lead score and campaign tracking.
- **Provider side** (optometrists.org, Zocdoc): clinics apply for leads or featured listings.
- **Giving** (BrightFocus, Seva): preset amounts, monthly option, stated purposes.
- **Contests** (EyeWiki): monthly quiz with prize draw and official rules.
- **Accessibility** (RNIB, Macular Society): text-size control and high-contrast theme.
- **Video** (AAO, NEI): embedded official videos, which AAO explicitly permits.
